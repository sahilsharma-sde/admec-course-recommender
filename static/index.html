Tips for better results
  1. Say it in your own words. “Become a video editor” or “start a career in data” works better than a single word.
  2. Name the tool or the job. Photoshop, AutoCAD, Python, SEO or “web designer”. Use whichever you already know.
  3. No match? Rephrase. Swap the tool for the job (“HTML” for “website designer”) or the other way round. The page tells you when nothing fits.
  4. Pick a level. Beginner shows shorter, entry-level courses and Advanced shows the deeper ones. Leave it on Any if you are not sure.
  5. Read the highlights. Skills in yellow are the ones that matched what you typed. “Also offered as” lists other versions of a course, and “Show similar courses” suggests related ones.
  6. Check the course page. The bar compares each result with the best match, so it is not a grade. Fees, batch timings and admission details are on the course page behind “View course”.

Your goal is used only to find matching courses and is not saved.
